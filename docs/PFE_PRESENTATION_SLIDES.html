1. Problem Statement & Field Challenges
Context

🚨 Single-API Fragility & Outages

  • Exhausted free-tier quotas: Disruptive 429 Too Many Requests errors during traffic peaks.
  • Single point of failure: Pl@ntNet or Gemini downtime immediately paralyzes the entire system.
  • Rural dead zones: Botanical field researchers (Sahara, Atlas) operate with zero cellular signal.

⚠️ Therapeutic Hazards & Hallucinations

  • Uncontrolled generative LLMs: Severe risk of hallucinating abortifacient or neurotoxic plants for vulnerable users.
  • Missing provenance: Blur between anecdotal folk traditions and clinically validated pharmacology.
  • Unstructured academic data: 155 university field records locked in raw, isolated spreadsheets.

3. Resilient Router: Zero-Downtime Failover
AI Engineering

⚙️ EWMA Scoring & Circuit Breakers

Each external provider is continuously tracked using Exponentially Weighted Moving Averages:

μ_t = (1 - α) · μ_{t-1} + α · Latency_obs
S_t = (1 - α) · S_{t-1} + α · I(Success)

  • Closed: Normal routing to primary vendor (Gemini, Pl@ntNet).
  • Open: Consecutive 429/timeouts trip breaker ➔ Traffic routed to fallback.
  • Half-Open: Probing recovery after exponential cooldown elapsed.

4. Therapeutic Engine: Zero-Violation Safety
Medical Ethics

🛡️ Deterministic Safety Filter

  • Pregnancy Safety: Absolute exclusion of all taxa carrying the pregnancy_avoid flag (e.g., wormwood, pennyroyal).
  • Pediatric Safeguard: Filtering out neurotoxic high-camphor essential oils for patients < 12 years.
  • LLM Sandboxing: The generative model only phrases pre-filtered botanical data. Zero hallucinated taxa possible.

📚 Evidence-Based Medicine (EBM) Grades

Every therapeutic recommendation is weighted by verified clinical provenance:

Grade A Randomized Controlled Clinical Trials documented.
Grade B Peer-reviewed in vivo animal pharmacology verified.
Grade C Centuries of documented ethnobotanical folk usage.
Grade D In vitro bioassay / Biochemical hypothesis.

Bioclimatology
